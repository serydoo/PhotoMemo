# MemoMark 2.1.0 App Store 更新说明

## 简体中文

根据大家近期的反馈，时光记迎来一次较大的体验更新，建议及时更新。

- 全新的四区卡片编辑体验，位置关系、实时预览和完成操作更清楚。
- 首页直接呈现重要日子的“今天时间答案”，设置与查看重要日子更直观。
- 配置中心、记忆对象和设置页面层级进一步整理，小屏阅读与操作更从容。
- 从 Apple Photos 分享、查看处理进展和返回相册的路径更明确。
- 选图、任务重试与购买操作获得更清楚的状态和失败提示。

照片仍然只在设备本地处理；原始照片保持不变，时光记只保存新的结果。

## English (U.S.)

Thanks to your recent feedback, MemoMark is receiving a major experience update. We recommend updating now.

- A redesigned four-region card editor makes position, live preview, and completion clearer.
- Home now shows today's time answer for an important date, with a more intuitive setup experience.
- Configuration, Memory Subject, and Settings use a clearer hierarchy, especially on smaller screens.
- Sharing from Apple Photos, checking progress, and returning to Photos now follow a clearer path.
- Photo picking, task retry, and purchase actions provide clearer states and failure messages.

Photos are still processed only on your device. Originals remain unchanged, and MemoMark saves a new result.
