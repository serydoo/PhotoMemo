# MemoMark 2.3.6 — App Store

## zh-Hans

新增 MemoMark+ 永久解锁选项，可在订阅下方购买或领取。修复部分 Live Photo 保存失败的问题，并改善重试提示。优化玻璃卡片的文字排版、呈现细节，以及配置时的预览和底部操作。照片在本地处理，原图保持不变。

## en-US

Added a lifetime unlock option for MemoMark+ below subscriptions. Fixed saving failures for some Live Photos and clarified retry guidance. Refined GlassCard typography and presentation, along with the configuration preview and bottom controls. Photos are processed locally, and originals remain unchanged.
