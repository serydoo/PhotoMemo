改善从“照片”分享后的处理衔接，修复部分处理结果中文字或标识未正常显示的问题，并避免已完成的历史任务重新显示为新的持续活动。照片在本地处理，原图保持不变。后台接续效果取决于系统版本与照片权限。

## U.S. English

Improves processing after sharing from Photos, fixes missing text or marks in some saved results, and prevents completed history from appearing as a new Live Activity. Processing stays on your device and originals remain unchanged. Background continuation depends on the system version and Photos permissions.
