## zh-Hans — Settings release notes

让常用内容更顺手，让每次回看更从容。

这次我们整理了横屏首页与进展，也让时间表达和保存状态更清楚。

### 横屏里，把常用内容放在一起

- 空间充足的大屏 iPhone 横屏中，记忆对象和我的预设并排呈现，标题与卡片顶部更整齐。
- 多个预设可以在右侧独立上下滑动，标题和管理入口留在上方；竖屏及较小屏幕保持熟悉的布局。

### 刚完成的回忆，更容易找到

- 大屏横屏中，刚刚完成的结果与最近保存并排显示，方便查看新照片与历史记录。
- 最近保存列表支持独立上下滑动，标题和操作入口保持可见。

### 同一个时间起点，换一种语气

- 精简时间表达的说明，围绕设定的时间锚点，选择自然、仪式感、成长、温馨或极简语气。
- 表达示例继续帮助你比较说法，是否在照片上加入时间表达，由卡片内容决定。

### 调整之后，更清楚地确认保存

- 改善配置修改与保存状态的衔接，让当前预设与已保存内容保持一致。
- 完善日期和称呼的显示；横屏配置页的预览开关放在主导航上方，保存和更多操作放在下方，切换页面时主导航保持原位。

### 从照片出发，再回到照片

- 改善从 Apple Photos 分享后的处理接续和状态显示；后台体验仍取决于系统版本、照片权限与运行状态。
- 照片继续在本地处理，原图保持不变；完成后的新记忆照片保存回 Apple Photos。

谢谢你把日常使用中的细节告诉我们。愿下一次打开照片时，那段回忆依然清晰。

## en — Settings release notes

Keep everyday actions close, and give each memory room to be revisited.

This update refines landscape Home and Progress, with clearer time-expression guidance and save states.

### Your everyday choices, side by side

- On larger iPhones with enough landscape space, your Memory Subject and Presets sit side by side with aligned headings and card tops.
- With multiple Presets, scroll the right column independently while its heading and management action stay visible. Portrait and smaller screens keep their familiar layout.

### Find the memory you just finished

- In supported large-phone landscape layouts, the latest result sits beside recently saved memories, making both easier to revisit.
- Scroll recent saves independently while the heading and actions remain visible.

### One time anchor, a different tone

- Shorter guidance helps you choose Natural, Ceremonial, Growth, Warm or Minimal time expressions around your chosen time anchor.
- Examples help you compare wording. Card Content still determines whether time expressions appear on your photo.

### Know when your changes are saved

- Improved coordination between configuration edits and save states keeps the current Preset aligned with its saved content.
- Refined dates and names. In landscape Configuration, the preview control sits above the main navigation, with Save and More below. Main navigation stays in place as you switch pages.

### From Photos, back to Photos

- Improved processing continuation and status presentation after sharing from Apple Photos. Background behavior still depends on the system version, Photos permissions and runtime conditions.
- Photos stay on your device, with the original unchanged. A new memory photo is saved back to Apple Photos when processing finishes.

Thank you for sharing the little details of everyday use. May each return to your photos bring the memory back clearly.
