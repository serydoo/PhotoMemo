# MemoMark 2.2.1 (90) App Store「此版本的新内容」

## 简体中文

- 新增更轻盈的「极简」卡片样式，让一段重要的回忆自然留在照片画面里。
- 动态照片会按原本的动态格式交还 Apple Photos；照片始终在设备本地处理，原图保持不变。
- 生日、纪念日和倒计时的时间表达更贴近日常生活；预览、配置和最终结果也更容易理解。
- 处理中断或重新打开应用后更容易恢复；照片仍在设备本地处理，原图保持不变。

## U.S. English

- Added a quieter Minimal card style for memories that need one focused line of context.
- Live Photos keep their motion when you choose to preserve their original format. Photos stay processed on device and originals remain unchanged.
- Time anchors now use clearer everyday language for birthdays, anniversaries, and countdowns, with better alignment between preview, configuration, and final output.
- Processing is easier to recover after an interruption or relaunch, while photos remain on-device and originals stay unchanged.
