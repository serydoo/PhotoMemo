## 审核说明（English）

MemoMark is a local-first memory presentation app built around Apple Photos. There is no account or test login. User-selected photos are processed on-device, the original asset is not modified, and a new memory photo is saved back to Apple Photos.

For the Card Content Editor, reviewers can verify empty, text-only, punctuation, module-only, and mixed text/module states. The line box and caret remain stable when a module is inserted, deleted, or reordered. The core workflow does not require a purchase; StoreKit review is optional from Settings → MemoMark+.

MemoMark does not upload photos, use third-party advertising, or include tracking. App Privacy and Export Compliance answers must be confirmed against the final binary in App Store Connect.
