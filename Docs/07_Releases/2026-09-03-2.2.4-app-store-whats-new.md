## 简体中文

```text
这次更新让时光记在配置、照片处理和恢复时更加稳定连贯：

• 优化记忆对象、预设、相册和卡片编辑之间的切换体验，较早返回的内容不会覆盖你当前的选择。
• 改进头像选择与裁切的取消和恢复路径，编辑过程更安心。
• 进一步优化照片保存、处理中断后的恢复和任务状态呈现。
• 静态照片和完整 Live Photo 继续按照各自的规则处理，完成后生成新的记忆照片保存回 Apple Photos。
• 持续整理本地处理流程，让日后的维护与稳定性改进更可靠。

照片仍只在设备本地处理，原图保持不变。
```

## U.S. English

```text
This update makes MemoMark more stable and consistent when configuring, processing, and recovering photos:

• Improve switching between Memory Subjects, presets, albums, and card editing so earlier results do not replace your current choice.
• Refine avatar selection and crop cancellation and recovery.
• Improve photo save recovery and task status after an interruption.
• Continue to handle still photos and complete Live Photos through their appropriate paths, saving a new memory photo back to Apple Photos.
• Continue refining the on-device processing foundation for more dependable maintenance and stability improvements.

Photos stay on your device and originals remain unchanged.
```
