# MemoMark 2.1.1 App Store 更新说明

## 简体中文

这次更新让卡片内容、自选标识和配置保存保持一致。

- 编辑、保存、重新进入或切换记忆对象与配置时，卡片文字和四个内容区域继续显示最新内容。
- Apple 小标识、对象头像和自选 Logo 保持正确来源，并在卡片预览和最终输出中统一呈现。
- 修复自选 Logo 保存、导入、恢复或切换配置后消失或错用头像的问题。
- 自选 Logo 的圆形选择、预览和最终呈现更加稳定。

照片仍然在设备本地处理，原始照片保持不变，时光记只保存新的结果。

## English (U.S.)

This update keeps card content, custom logos, and saved configurations in sync.

- Card text and all four content regions remain current after editing, saving, reopening, or switching subjects and configurations.
- The Apple mini logo, Subject Avatar, and custom Logo keep their intended sources across card preview and final output.
- Fixed custom logos disappearing or using an avatar after saving, importing, restoring, or switching configurations.
- Custom Logo selection, preview, and final output now keep a more consistent circular appearance.

Photos remain processed on device. Originals stay unchanged, and MemoMark saves a new result.
