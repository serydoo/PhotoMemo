# MemoMark 2.1.2 App Store 更新说明

## 简体中文

这次维护让照片处理结果更清楚，也让需要处理的照片更容易找到。

- 没有可呈现内容的照片不会被保存成空白结果。
- 批量处理会清楚区分已完成的照片和需要处理的照片。
- 处理需要关注时，可以从通知直接回到对应状态。

照片仍然在设备本地处理，原始照片保持不变，时光记只保存新的结果。

## English (U.S.)

This maintenance update makes photo-processing results clearer and makes photos that need attention easier to find.

- Photos with no presentable content are no longer saved as blank results.
- Batch processing clearly separates completed photos from those that need attention.
- When a result needs attention, a notification can take you back to the related status.

Photos remain processed on device. Originals stay unchanged, and MemoMark saves a new result.
