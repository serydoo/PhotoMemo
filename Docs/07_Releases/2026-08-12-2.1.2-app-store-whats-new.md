# MemoMark 2.1.2 App Store 更新说明

## 简体中文

这次更新让记忆对象、预设和处理结果保持一致。

- 修复编辑记忆对象后重新进入或切换时出现旧内容的问题，并改进头像裁切与圆形预览。
- 时间锚点、记忆卡内容、输出设置和 Logo 会随当前对象一起更新。
- 自选标识统一为圆形，并修复保存后从预览和最终呈现中消失的问题。
- 进展页现在显示真实结果缩略图；多张照片使用稳定代表图和数量提示。
- 首页现在会简短说明如何从 Apple Photos 开始日常记录。

照片仍在设备本地处理；原始照片保持不变，时光记只保存新的结果。

## English (U.S.)

This update keeps Memory Subjects, presets, and finished results in sync.

- Fixed stale subject details after editing, reopening, or switching, with improved avatar cropping and circular previews.
- Time anchors, card content, output settings, and logos now follow the current subject together.
- Custom logos use a consistent circular appearance and no longer disappear after saving.
- Progress now shows real result thumbnails, with a stable representative and count for multi-photo jobs.
- Home now briefly explains the everyday Apple Photos workflow.

Photos remain processed on device. Originals stay unchanged, and MemoMark saves a new result.
