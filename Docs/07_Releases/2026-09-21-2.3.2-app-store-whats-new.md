# MemoMark 2.3.2（108）App Store 版本更新说明

状态：`Draft`；本文件只准备商店文案，未提交 App Store Connect。

## 简体中文

这次更新继续推进胶片时间（FilmMark）的开发，并让配置中心的样式选择、预览与输出更一致。新建预设后，所选的经典白、极简或胶片时间样式会更可靠地延续到最终结果；保存与再次保存的状态也更清楚。照片继续只在设备本地处理，原始照片保持不变。

## U.S. English

This update continues the development of FilmMark while making Configuration Center style choices, preview, and output more consistent. The Classic White, Minimal, or Film Time style you choose for a new preset now stays aligned through the final result, with clearer save and retry states. Photos continue to be processed on device, while originals remain unchanged.
