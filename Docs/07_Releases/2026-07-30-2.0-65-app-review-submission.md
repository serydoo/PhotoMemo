# MemoMark 2.0 (65) App Store 提交文案

日期：2026 年 7 月 30 日

提交版本：`2.0 (65)`
适用范围：App Store Connect 的“此版本的新功能”、审核备注与拒审回复

## 使用说明

本次文案合并了 `2.0.1 (48)` 中已完成的稳定性与体验改进，以及本次对
MemoMark+ 购买入口的审核修复。它只陈述已实现的用户体验和 StoreKit 调用路径；
不将本地构建、设备安装或空商品返回表述为 Sandbox 交易成功。

## 此版本的新功能（简体中文）

```text
本次更新让记录、设置和分享后的处理更加可靠，也改善了 MemoMark+ 的购买体验：

• 改进从 Apple Photos 分享后保存、恢复与失败处理的稳定性。
• 优化配置保存、记忆对象切换，以及预览与最终结果的一致性。
• 改善 iPhone 上的设置层级、小屏排版与中英文体验。
• 修复 MemoMark+ 购买入口：现在会请求 App Store；服务暂时不可用时会给出明确说明，并可重新尝试。

照片仍只在设备本地处理，不会修改 Apple Photos 中的原始照片。
```

## What's New（English）

```text
This update makes creating and keeping your memories more reliable, and improves the MemoMark+ purchase experience:

• Improves saving, recovery, and failure handling after sharing from Apple Photos.
• Makes saved settings, Memory Subject changes, previews, and final results more consistent.
• Refines Settings hierarchy, compact iPhone layouts, and Chinese and English language support.
• Fixes the MemoMark+ purchase entry: it now requests the App Store and clearly explains when the service is temporarily unavailable, with a retry option.

Photos are still processed only on your device, and original photos in Apple Photos remain unchanged.
```

## 审核备注（English）

```text
Guideline 2.1(b) resolution

MemoMark+ is a non-consumable purchase that permanently unlocks additional recording capacity.

Review path: Settings → MemoMark+ → permanent unlock action.

Selecting the action invokes StoreKit 2 Product.purchase() for
com.serydoo.PhotoMemo.iOS.memomarkplus.lifetime. The same StoreKit purchase path is used in Sandbox and production; no sign-in, local activation, or special reviewer steps are required.

If StoreKit is temporarily unavailable, the app now shows an explicit unavailable state and a retry action rather than accepting the tap without feedback. Restore Purchases is available on the same MemoMark+ page.
```

## 拒审回复（English）

```text
Hello App Review Team,

We addressed the In-App Purchase issue reported for version 2.0.

MemoMark+ is a non-consumable purchase that permanently unlocks additional recording capacity. The purchase path is Settings → MemoMark+ → permanent unlock action. The action now always invokes StoreKit 2 Product.purchase() for com.serydoo.PhotoMemo.iOS.memomarkplus.lifetime, including in the Sandbox environment used for review.

If the App Store service is temporarily unavailable, the app displays a clear explanation and a retry action instead of accepting the tap without feedback. No account or special test steps are required.

Thank you for your review.
```
