# MemoMark 2.0 App Store 更新说明

适用版本：`2.0`

用途：App Store Connect -> 新版本 -> 更新说明（What's New）

以下两段为面向用户的发布文案，可直接粘贴到对应本地化字段。不要将内部测试、构建号、价格、首批名额或技术实现细节写入 App Store 更新说明。

## 简体中文

MemoMark 2.0 来了。

- 全新推出 MemoMark+：一次购买，持续记录更多未来值得回看的瞬间。
- 支持购买、恢复购买与兑换权益；已激活用户可在设置中查看 MemoMark+ 状态。
- 优化批量处理体验：MemoMark+ 可一次处理更多照片。
- 显著提升后台处理与写入照片图库的稳定性，减少任务停留和重复保存的情况。
- 优化输出文件命名：同一张照片多次处理时，编号会自动延续。
- 设置现已支持更清晰的语言切换与更完整的反馈渠道。

所有照片仍仅在你的设备上处理，不会上传；原始照片始终保留不变。

## English (U.S.)

MemoMark 2.0 is here.

- Introducing MemoMark+: a one-time purchase for keeping more moments worth revisiting.
- Purchase, restore, and redemption are now supported. Activated users can view their MemoMark+ status in Settings.
- Improved batch processing: MemoMark+ can process more photos in a single batch.
- Improved background processing and Photo Library saving reliability, reducing stalled tasks and duplicate saves.
- Improved output naming: repeated edits of the same photo now continue their sequence automatically.
- Settings now offers clearer language selection and more complete feedback options.

Your photos are still processed only on your device and are never uploaded. Originals always remain unchanged.
