# MemoMark 2.1.1 App Store 更新说明

## 简体中文

这次更新让记忆对象切换更加连贯。

- 切换对象后，时间锚点、记忆卡预览、Logo、输出设置和编辑内容会同步更新。
- 已保存配置与待保存草稿的状态显示更加准确。
- 修复对象切换后部分页面仍显示上一对象内容的问题。

照片仍然只在设备本地处理；原始照片保持不变，时光记只保存新的结果。

## English (U.S.)

This update makes switching between Memory Subjects more consistent.

- Time anchors, card previews, logos, output settings, and editing content now refresh together.
- Saved configurations and unsaved subject drafts are represented more accurately.
- Fixed cases where parts of the interface could continue showing the previous subject after switching.

Photos are still processed only on your device. Originals remain unchanged, and MemoMark saves a new result.
