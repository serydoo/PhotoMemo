## Product Hunt

### Product name

MemoMark — Memory cards from real photos and time

### Tagline

Create a memory card from a real photo, while keeping the original intact.

### Description

MemoMark is a local-first Apple app for presenting memories. Start with a photo from Apple Photos, choose the person and time that matter, compose your own words and photo information, and save a new memory card back to your photo workflow. Your original photo stays untouched, and MemoMark is designed around on-device processing rather than uploading private photos to a cloud service.

### First comment from the maker

I built MemoMark because a photo can tell us when something happened, but not always where that moment belongs in a person's life. MemoMark keeps the original photo safe and gives the memory a small, intentional place to be expressed. I would especially value feedback from people who keep family photos, use Apple Photos every day, or care about local-first tools.

## Indie Hackers

### Title

Building a local-first memory presentation app around real photo time

### Post

I am building MemoMark, a native Apple app for people who want their photos to keep more of the life around them. It starts from a real photo in Apple Photos and lets the user shape a memory around a person, a time anchor, and their own words.

The product deliberately does not try to become a cloud photo manager or an AI storyteller. The original photo remains untouched, and the workflow is designed around local processing and a new saved image. The hard part is not adding another filter; it is preserving the truth of the capture time while expressing where that photo belongs in someone's life.

MemoMark is currently in a research-led refinement stage. I am studying how different expression styles should work inside a preset without turning the product into a template marketplace. I am looking for feedback from independent builders, Apple users, and anyone who has tried to keep a meaningful family or personal photo timeline.

## 公共中文短文 / 社交简介

### 版本 A：产品介绍

时光记从 Apple Photos 里的一张真实照片开始。你可以围绕一个人、一个时间起点和自己的文字，让回忆拥有属于自己的表达方式。原图不被修改，照片也不需要离开设备。

### 版本 B：隐私角度

有些照片不应该先上传到云端，才有资格被好好保存。时光记坚持本地优先：从 Apple Photos 分享，完成记忆呈现，再把新的作品保存回照片工作流。

### 版本 C：开发者角度

我正在做 MemoMark（时光记）：不是给照片加水印，而是研究如何用真实拍摄时间和人生锚点，把照片呈现成一张可以长期保存的记忆卡片。
