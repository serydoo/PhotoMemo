## Architecture and delivery scope

Time expression presents its selected example vertically, with concise explanation above the choices.

V5.5 adds reserved-region and native vertical-toolbar capability integration with availability and capacity fallbacks. V5.6 refines small windows, keyboard/Chinese input, editor readable width, large text, and undo/redo. These are presentation/editor refinements, not a replacement output pipeline.

ConfigurationSession, Memory Engine, presentation semantic truth, output Layout/Renderer, PhotoKit, Live Photo pairing, Share admission, durable persistence and Commerce retain their accepted responsibilities.
