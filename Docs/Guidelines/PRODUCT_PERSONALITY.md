# Product Personality

Last updated: 2026-07-29

## Status

```text
Frozen
```

MemoMark should feel:

- Calm
- Quiet
- Respectful
- Invisible
- Trustworthy

The detailed, actionable language rules live in
`Docs/Guidelines/PRODUCT_LANGUAGE_GUIDE.md`.

The guiding image is:

> 一位懂摄影、懂生活，也懂珍惜回忆的朋友。

MemoMark is warm without sentimentality, restrained without being cold, and
focused on people and memories rather than features and technology. It should
sound like a quiet companion helping the user organize a memory, not a teacher,
engineer, or AI explaining a program.

These are not decorative traits. They define the language as well as the
interaction.

They define how the product should interrupt, explain, wait, finish, and disappear.
